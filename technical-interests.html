My Technical Interests

As a Computer Science student at the University of Hertfordshire, I am interested in learning about different areas of technology. I want to understand how computer systems work and how technology can solve everyday problems. Two areas that particularly interest me are web development and cybersecurity.

Web Development

Web development is one of my main technical interests because I enjoy learning how websites are created and how people interact with them. I am interested in understanding how HTML is used to structure web pages and how CSS is used to improve their appearance and layout.

Through my university studies and personal learning, I hope to improve my web development skills and understand how to create websites that are simple, accessible and easy to use. I am also interested in learning more about responsive design so that websites work properly on computers, tablets and mobile phones.

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is another area of computing that interests me. As more people use computers and online services, protecting personal information and digital systems has become increasingly important. I want to learn how cybersecurity helps protect computers, networks and information from online threats.

I am particularly interested in understanding common security risks, such as phishing, weak passwords and unauthorised access. In the future, I would like to develop my knowledge of network security and learn how organisations can protect their systems and users. I believe cybersecurity is an important part of modern computing.
